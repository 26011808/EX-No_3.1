EX. NO. 3.1. CREATE 3D MODEL FROM ORTHOGRAPHIC PROJECTION

AIM:

To create a 3D model from given orthographic projection.

REQUIREMENTS:
1. Autodesk Fusion 360
2. Windows 10
3. 16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 1 GB or more)
4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload

PROCEDURE

Step 1: Study the Given Diagram
1. Carefully observe the given engineering drawing.
2. Identify the three main views: Top View, Front View and Right Side View.
3. Note all the important dimensions, holes, slots, rectangular portions and curved surfaces.
4. Understand the position and relationship between the three views.
5. Select a suitable drawing scale, preferably 1:1, if the drawing sheet permits.

Step 2: Draw the Front View
1. Draw a horizontal reference line for the Front View.
2. Mark the centre point of the rounded portion.
3. Draw a semicircular end with a radius of 16 mm.
4. Draw a circular hole of diameter 16 mm (Ø16) at the centre of the rounded end.
5. Extend the horizontal lines to form the rectangular main body.
6. Mark a distance of 36 mm from the hole centre to the shoulder.
7. Draw the extended rectangular portion with a length of 32 mm.
8. Draw the vertical boundary lines to complete the main body.
9. Add a centre line through the circular hole.
10. Draw hidden lines to represent the internal edges.

Step 3: Draw the Top View
1. Draw the Top View above the Front View.
2. Project the required vertical construction lines from the Front View.
3. Mark the overall width as 40 mm.
4. Divide the width into three portions: 11 mm, 18 mm and 11 mm.
5. Draw the upper and lower rectangular portions.
6. Construct the central pen slot with a width of 18 mm.
7. Mark the horizontal dimensions according to the given drawing.
8. Draw the extended rectangular portion on the right side.
9. Represent the hidden edges using dashed lines.
10. Draw the necessary centre lines to indicate the positions of the holes.

Step 4: Draw the Right Side View
1. Draw the Right Side View on the right side of the Front View.
2. Project the necessary horizontal lines from the Front View.
3. Draw the outer rectangular boundary with a width of 40 mm and a height of 32 mm.
4. Mark the centre point of the view.
5. Draw a circular hole with a diameter of 18 mm (Ø18).
6. Draw horizontal and vertical centre lines passing through the circular hole.
7. Represent the internal edges using hidden (dashed) lines.
8. Complete the Right Side View according to the given dimensions.

Step 5: Finishing the Drawing
1. Check the alignment of all three orthographic views.
2. Darken the visible outlines using a suitable pencil.
3. Draw hidden edges using dashed lines.
4. Draw centre lines using thin chain lines.
5. Add all necessary dimensions, arrows and extension lines.
6. Erase unwanted construction lines.
7. Check the accuracy and neatness of the completed drawing.
